The following catalog presents circumstances for every solar eclipse visible from 
Washington
 during the period 
2001 CE to 3000 CE
. 

For convenience, all dates and times are given in local standard time 
(= GMT -  5.0)
.
Note that if daylight savings time is in effect, then one hour must be added to the times. 
In addition to the date and the type of eclipse visible from 
Washington
, the local times for the beginning, maximum and end of the eclipse are given. 
For maximum eclipse, each catalog lists the Sun's altitude and azimuth as well as the magnitude (fraction of Sun's diameter covered) 
and obscuration (fraction of Sun's area covered). 
The number of years elapsed since the previous eclipse visible from 
Washington
 is listed next.
If the eclipse is annular or total from some portion of the Earth's surface, then the distance (km) from 
Washington
 to the center and edge of the eclipse path are provided. 


Special notes (last column) call attention to major partial eclipses (magnitude ≥ 
 0.75
), 
or occasions when a total or annular eclipse path passes within 
  500 km
 of 
Washington
. Finally, if the eclipse is actually annular or total from 
Washington
, then the duration of annularity or totality is given.
For a complete description of each item in the catalog, please see Key to Solar Eclipse Viewing Circumstances. 
For a basic explanation on why solar eclipses occur, see Eclipses for Beginners.
