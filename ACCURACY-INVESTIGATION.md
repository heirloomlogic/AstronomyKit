## Remaining Saturn discrepancy

For A11/0 (2041-02-01T16:02:08.237915Z):

- Native delta-T is 85.563067 s; Swiss delta-T is 72.089644 s, a 13.473423 s
  difference. Common-TT comparison still leaves about 62.6 s between the native
  station and the root of Swiss's returned `SPEED`.
- At common TT, native apparent longitude differs by only 0.267797 arcseconds.
  A small position error does not establish station-time accuracy.
- Removing aberration and then light travel from **both** diagnostic paths still
  leaves approximately 62.2 s and 62.1 s of station disagreement. These component
  comparisons do not isolate a large apparent-correction defect.
- Swiss's returned `SPEED` reproduces the frozen event within 0.02 s. Differencing
  Swiss's own apparent longitudes over widths 0.007–0.14 day instead places its
  speed-zero root roughly 53.7–55.3 s earlier. Very narrow differences are noisier.
  This is evidence of a material distinction between the reference's returned
  speed and differentiated longitude, not authorization to replace its event.

The latter distinction must be independently assessed before choosing another
model correction. The current measurements do not prove the Swiss reference is
wrong or establish which station time is physically preferable. No fitted speed
or time offset, altered delta-T default, reference substitution, or tolerance
change has been applied.

The full nutation calculation matches the independent
[ERFA `t_nut00b` reference](https://github.com/liberfa/erfa/blob/master/src/t_erfa_c.c)
at its published 1e-13-radian tolerance.

Raw results and logs are retained under [Scripts/accuracy/results](Scripts/accuracy/results).

repository revision and byte hashes. Component output carries the oracle binary
and version. Local probes establish accuracy at known roots, not broad search
completeness or release readiness.

The next reviewed decision is how to reconcile **returned reference speeds versus
differentiated apparent longitude**, and what remaining model/time corrections
are justified by independent evidence. Preserve A11/0 and the 60-second gate
through that investigation. Once all 36 pass, resume the planned 1900–2100
validation, JPL cross-checks, full platform/sanitizer and performance qualification,
major release preparation, and paired AstrologyKit observation migration.

this work does not complete its 74-case adapter or certified search service.

## Independent review of this investigation

Review converged in **2 rounds**. One artifact-provenance defect was fixed:
the component report was rerun against the exact final matrix library, and a
failing-first regression now verifies their archived binary hashes agree.
The regenerated component report changed only its library hash. The narrow
second review independently passed all seven diagnostic tests and found no
remaining review issues. This accepts the investigation artifacts, not the
candidate's failed astronomical gate.

Review requested Astra/high for both Critics and Terra/medium for the localized
fix; dispatch accepted those selections. No escalation or capability retry was
needed. Runtime model confirmation and actual usage were not exposed.
